### 📌 Overview & SEO Summary

Welcome to the ultimate curated directory of **cloud purchasing and procurement platforms**, **open-source e-procurement systems**, and **spend management frameworks**. ## 🔓 Open-Source GitHub Projects

*Sorted by GitHub_Stars_Count (Descending)* 🌟

- **[Odoo](https://github.com/odoo/odoo)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/odoo/odoo?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/odoo/odoo/stargazers) 🌟  
  **World's most popular open-source ERP suite**, LGPL-3.0 licensed. **Comprehensive Purchase & Procurement Module**: automated RFQs, purchase orders, vendor price lists, 3-way matching, replenishment rules, and multi-currency vendor bill management. 🏢

- **[ERPNext](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/frappe/erpnext?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext/stargazers) 🌟  
  **100% open-source enterprise ERP platform**, GPL-3.0 licensed. **Complete Buying & Procurement Suite**: Material Requests, Supplier Quotations, Purchase Orders, Goods Received Notes (GRN), Supplier Scorecards, 3-way invoice matching, and purchase analytics. 📦

- **[MyCompany](https://github.com/lsfusion-solutions/mycompany)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/lsfusion-solutions/mycompany?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/lsfusion-solutions/mycompany/stargazers) 🌟  
  **Free, open-source, self-hosted ERP and CRM for small businesses**, Apache-2.0 licensed. **Single database for all modules** — no synchronization between parts of the system. **Purchasing & Inventory**: purchase orders, multi-warehouse stock, receipts, shipments, and movement history. Runs on **4 GB RAM server** with Docker. 🏢

- **[OpenProcurement API](https://github.com/openprocurement/openprocurement.api)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/openprocurement/openprocurement.api?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/openprocurement/openprocurement.api/stargazers) 🌟  
  **Core RESTful API powering Ukraine's Prozorro national e-procurement system**, Apache-2.0 licensed. **Open Contracting Data Standard (OCDS) compliant**: handles public tenders, automated auctions, bid evaluation, supplier contracts, and public procurement transparency at sovereign state scale. 🌐

- **[EasyPO-Form](https://github.com/Dorcas-Detalles/EasyPO-Form)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/Dorcas-Detalles/EasyPO-Form?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/Dorcas-Detalles/EasyPO-Form/stargazers) 🌟  
  **Modern headless Purchase Order management system**, open-source. **Built on .NET 9 and Vue.js with Clean Architecture**: manages purchase order forms, itemized requisitions, vendor details, and status workflows with clean RESTful API contracts. 📝

- **[eProcurement Ontology (ePO)](https://github.com/OP-TED/ePO)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/OP-TED/ePO?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/OP-TED/ePO/stargazers) 🌟  
  **European Union public procurement formal semantic ontology**, open-source. **Maintained by the Publications Office of the EU**: defines conceptual models and linked data standards for end-to-end e-procurement data harmonization across European member states. 🇪🇺

- **[Inventory & Order Management System](https://github.com/FaroukOyekunle/Inventory-Order-Management-System)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/FaroukOyekunle/Inventory-Order-Management-System?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/FaroukOyekunle/Inventory-Order-Management-System/stargazers) 🌟  
  **ASP.NET Core MVC inventory and procurement system**, open-source. **End-to-end purchasing workflow**: manages suppliers, product catalogs, purchase orders, inventory restocking, and order status fulfillment tracking. 📦

- **[e-Procure](https://github.com/Gichure/e-procure)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/Gichure/e-procure?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/Gichure/e-procure/stargazers) 🌟  
  **Microservice e-procurement platform**, open-source. **Built with Spring Boot and Camunda BPMN engine**: automated procurement workflows, purchase request approvals, tendering, and Kubernetes container orchestration. ⚙️

- **[Procuman Community Edition](https://github.com/procuman/procuman-community-edition)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/procuman/procuman-community-edition?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/procuman/procuman-community-edition/stargazers) 🌟  
  **Free e-Procurement software built on EspoCRM**, open-source. **Supplier management** with contact database and categorization. Product catalog with supplier associations, purchase order management with automated calculations and PDF generation. 🛒

- **[VendorBridge AI](https://github.com/vatsalgajera-tech/VendorBridge-AI)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/vatsalgajera-tech/VendorBridge-AI?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/vatsalgajera-tech/VendorBridge-AI/stargazers) 🌟  
  **AI-powered procurement ERP**, open-source. **AI vendor scoring**: ranks vendors on price competitiveness, delivery speed, and auto-recommends optimal supplier with justification. RFQs, multi-stage approvals, and auto PO generation. 🤖

- **[Procure-to-Pay System](https://github.com/manziosee/procure-to-pay-system)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/manziosee/procure-to-pay-system?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/manziosee/procure-to-pay-system/stargazers) 🌟  
  **Django REST API & React TypeScript P2P system**, open-source. **Dual approval workflows**: Staff request → Level 1 & Level 2 Approvers → Auto PO generation → Receipt validation. AI OCR invoice extraction via Tesseract and OpenAI. 📋

- **[BrassLedger](https://github.com/rhamenator/BrassLedger)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/rhamenator/BrassLedger?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/rhamenator/BrassLedger/stargazers) 🌟  
  **Cross-platform accounting and business management system**, GPL-3.0 licensed. Cross-platform .NET application covering general ledger, payables, receivables, and procurement operation workspaces. 💼

- **[Nexus Procurement](https://github.com/azaharizaman/nexus-procurement)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/azaharizaman/nexus-procurement?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/azaharizaman/nexus-procurement/stargazers) 🌟  
  **Framework-agnostic procurement management package**, MIT licensed. Pure PHP 8.3+ engine with zero dependencies. Purchase requisitions, PO budget validation, GRN, and **3-way matching engine (<500ms for 100 lines)**. 🏛️

- **[Nexus Payable](https://github.com/azaharizaman/nexus-payable)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/azaharizaman/nexus-payable?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/azaharizaman/nexus-payable/stargazers) 🌟  
  **Framework-agnostic accounts payable management package**, MIT licensed. Accounts payable engine with configurable vendor matching tolerances, multi-currency processing, and early payment discount tracking. 📊

- **[Vendor Management System](https://github.com/SevenSquare-Tech/vendor-management-system)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/SevenSquare-Tech/vendor-management-system?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/SevenSquare-Tech/vendor-management-system/stargazers) 🌟  
  **Desktop vendor management application**, open-source. Built with Laravel and Electron JS for vendor profiling, contract renewal tracking, and purchase order status management. 🏢

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement. ℹ️
- **Open-source procurement tools are not turnkey** — **Nexus Procurement requires PHP 8.3+ and Composer integration**. **Procuman requires EspoCRM platform installation**. **MyCompany requires 4 GB RAM and Docker or Linux server**. **Always validate procurement workflows with a proof-of-concept** before production deployment. ⚙️
- **3-way matching is critical for financial controls** — **Nexus Procurement validates Invoice-PO-GRN alignment** with **per-vendor tolerance configuration**. **Segregation of Duties is enforced**: Requester ≠ Approver ≠ Receiver ≠ Payment Authorizer. **These controls prevent fraud and duplicate payments**. 🛡️
- **Ukraine's Prozorro demonstrates open-source procurement at national scale** — **$6 billion in savings** and **30% drone price reduction** during wartime procurement. **The system is built on Open Contracting Data Standard** for cross-country data comparison. 🇺🇦
- **AI-powered procurement is emerging** — **VendorBridge AI auto-recommends vendors with justification**. **Procure-to-Pay System uses OpenAI for document extraction**. **Always validate AI recommendations against procurement policies** before acting. 🛒
